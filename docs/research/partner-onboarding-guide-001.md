# Partner RC 001: synthetic institution walkthrough

This release is for a controlled synthetic demonstration. Do not upload recordings, vocabulary, identities or documents from real participants. Accepted research intake is not verified language evidence, training permission or canonical approval.

## Join and establish the workspace

Open the invitation issued to your verified email address, sign in, then accept. Use **Forgot password** if needed; recovery cannot restore a membership your institution has removed. Invitations expire after seven days. Ask your administrator for a replacement if the link expired or was replaced. Never forward private links.

Complete your organization profile in **Administration**. Propose a language if the registry does not contain it; collection planning does not create a verified registry identity. Select the precise language/variety for the project. Two institutions can reference the same registry identity while keeping their projects and contributions private. Dialects are not blended.

Invite project collaborators with the minimum appropriate role: contributor, researcher or PI. An organization administrator manages institutional membership; project invitations share only that project. Review pending invitations, expiry and delivery status in Administration. Mock delivery provides a private preview link and sends no email. **Send/resend invitation** replaces the previous link; **Revoke** prevents acceptance. Provider acceptance does not guarantee inbox delivery. A second organization administrator must accept before the last administrator can be removed.

## Collect and review

Create a campaign and select a category and required/optional questions. In **Collect**, save a synthetic draft and choose each allowed use separately. Resume the saved draft before submitting. Required fields and internal research/human-review permission must be completed. Saving a draft does not give other contributors access.

Attach a synthetic UTF-8 text, PDF, WAV or WebM file up to 5 MB. Files remain quarantined until a worker scans them. Pending, infected and failed scans cannot be downloaded. A **synthetic mock scan** label is a test result, not malware certification. If processing remains pending, ask the operator to check the scanner; do not repeatedly upload the same material. Browser recording needs microphone permission and supported WebM recording; file upload is the fallback.

Optional assistance displays machine-generated, unverified suggestions separately from original material. Originals are preserved. External AI is disabled by default and also requires project authorization and contributor permission. Unsupported transcription and invented translations are not accepted. No AI suggestion automatically becomes verified evidence.

Researchers and PIs use **Intake** to request clarification, reject, or accept for research intake. Contributors can revise a clarification request and resubmit. The interface distinguishes original material, awaiting human review, intake acceptance and AI suggestions. Handoff remains blocked pending separate governed integration; acceptance does not publish or train anything.

## Withdraw, delete and resolve problems

Withdraw a contribution to stop attachment access and new processing. You may then **Request retained-data deletion**. The PI checks applicable holds and approves; the synthetic policy waits at least seven days before an operator-run purge. The status shows eligibility and progress. An approved hold pauses deletion before purge begins. Minimal rights, identity and hash receipts remain; backup expiry follows a separately approved policy.

For a conflict, refresh before editing again. For a session error, sign in again. If your membership was revoked, ask an authorized administrator to review access. For a delivery/scan error, report the status and request ID to the operator without copying private content or invitation tokens. After a failed email delivery, an explicit resend creates a replacement link. Sign out on shared devices.

Local demonstration credentials and setup are in [the operator setup](institutional-data-studio-setup-001.md). They are synthetic-only and must never be reused on hosted staging. Operational limitations and deployment gates are in [the release runbook](../operations/partner-release-runbook-001.md).
