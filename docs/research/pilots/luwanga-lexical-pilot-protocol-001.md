# Luwanga canonical lexical pilot protocol 001

Version 1. Prepared 2026-11-19 (America/New_York). Status: protocol for independent review; no human approval recorded. See [readiness](luwanga-lexical-pilot-readiness-001.md), [packet specification](luwanga-lexical-evidence-manifest-spec-001.md), [qualifications](luwanga-reviewer-qualification-packet-001.md), [security triage](../../security/legacy-advisor-triage-001.md), and [validation](luwanga-lexical-pilot-validation-001.md).

## Purpose and boundaries

Determine whether authentic, appropriately permitted Luwanga evidence can support traceable, independently reviewed canonical lexical decisions through the existing pipeline. This is a bounded case study, not an estimate of translation accuracy, dialect-wide coverage, model performance, or training readiness. This document authorizes no import, promotion, deployment, qualification grant, recruitment, or public service.

The units of analysis are lexical identities, their forms and senses, and claim-level attestations. A dictionary row, spelling, English gloss, generated candidate or verified source quotation is not automatically a lexical identity or a true linguistic claim. Preserve exact forms, tone marks, spelling and locators. Record editorial interpretations separately. Never manufacture a native equivalent: use verified native or established borrowed terms; otherwise preserve the original term with a qualified explanation, or defer when uncertain.

## Sampling and eligibility

Purposefully select at most 12 lexical units after rights, provenance and scope gates pass. Count a polysemous lexeme once, while reporting its senses and attestations separately. Homonymous identities require an explicit identity decision. Prefer variation in sense, spelling, historical usage, established borrowing and disputed interpretation only where evidence supports it. No category quota, random-population inference or fabricated filler. Zero is the present eligible sample.

Maintain a private screening ledger with pseudonymous unit key, exact source/version/entry/evidence/artifact references, gate outcomes, reasons, assessor and date. Use the five outcomes: Eligible for pilot consideration; Pending rights confirmation; Pending linguistic verification; Pending provenance resolution; Excluded. Retain all simultaneous blockers; a primary outcome is not a statement that other gates passed. Exclude wrong-scope and machine-only claims. Re-screen changed records as a new version, retaining the old decision.

Admission requires a registered artifact linked to the source version; import checksum matching that artifact; an immutable source entry and matching evidence locator; current attributed human verification; exact primary Luwanga evidence and verified applicable source-label mapping; no unresolved competing primary dialect; disclosed contrary/qualifying evidence; current compatible rights and explicit INTERNAL_RESEARCH and HUMAN_REVIEW allowance. The chosen review channel also requires REVIEWER_DISPLAY authorization. Check expiration, supersession, ambiguity and disputes. Source possession, citation or research access alone cannot clear this gate.

Luwanga uses registry ID `928f469a-360c-49f1-8af2-006165673090`. The separate Wanga row is not an alias authorization. A source label must resolve through verified source-scoped assertions. No blending of Lutsotso, Bukusu, Luwanga, Maragoli, Isukha or other varieties; no parent-language inheritance. Comparative evidence cannot be relabeled primary Luwanga support to satisfy the pilot.

## Review and decision sequence

1. A source curator verifies provenance, exact transcription and research permissions in the existing source/evidence system. Preserve rights evidence privately. This is evidence verification, not canonical approval.
2. The proposer documents identity, form and sense decisions, alternatives and uncertainty in a versioned private packet. Candidate-generation suggestions remain hypotheses. Freeze the Migration 012 manifest; any amendment requires a new immutable proposal/version.
3. An independent qualified Luwanga reviewer records source fidelity, lexical identity, sense separation, variety scope, borrowing/historical justification and contradictions before seeing machine confidence/buckets. Record approve, revise, reject or uncertain for each claim with reasons. Do not repurpose or alter frozen HE001 study items or annotations.
4. A currently authorized canonical approver, distinct from the proposer, reviews the complete frozen evidence digest and judgments. The qualified independent reviewer may also be the approver if both responsibilities and authority are explicitly evidenced; record this arrangement. Disagreement triggers another qualified assessment or deferral, never automatic majority or machine resolution. The current portal cannot represent arbitrary lexical evidence targets without a separately reviewed extension; do not force them into dictionary-entry evaluation items.
5. A separately authorized future operator runs the existing dry-run, obtains independent approval, and performs a separately authorized atomic execution. Approval alone must create no canonical rows. This preparation stops before all real-data state transitions.
6. Inspect outputs, before/after states and events against the reviewed manifest; verify idempotency and expected pointers. Archive the audit in approved private storage with access/retention controls.

Rejected, withdrawn and disputed proposals remain in immutable governance history. Corrected proposals receive new IDs and idempotency keys. Disputing a completed proposal does not automatically change its outputs: any canonical dispute action needs its own reviewed proposal. Retain contrary attestations and earlier sense revisions; never erase disagreement to improve apparent agreement.

## Evaluation and success criteria

Report actual denominators: screened units, eligible units, deferred/excluded units, proposed claims, reviewed claims, disagreements and approved/executed claims. Report overlapping blocker counts separately. No percentage when the denominator is zero. With a small purposeful sample, report raw agreement and claim-specific disagreement reasons, not population accuracy.

| Measure | Required condition before a future pilot is called successful |
|---|---|
| Provenance | Every admitted claim traces through entry, matching locator, import, artifact digest and version |
| Source fidelity and scope | Every promoted form/interpretation has an explicit qualified judgment; exact Luwanga scope; no invented equivalent |
| Rights | Every actual activity is currently authorized; no training, redistribution or commercial inference |
| Human decisions | Every approved proposal has independent, valid, exact-scope authority and complete assessments |
| Contradictions | All known relevant contrary evidence retained; unresolved identity/meaning prevents approval |
| Integrity | No partial execution, unauthorized write, stale-digest acceptance or duplicate output |
| Audit | Every decision and output reproducible from frozen references and governed history |
| Confidentiality | No private text, identities, permissions or credentials in GitHub, public views, logs or shared caches |

Any confidentiality breach, lost audit, authority failure, unknown permission or invalid evidence pauses the affected workflow for investigation. Software tests establish mechanics only. Linguistic success requires actual human findings; zero eligible units means feasibility remains untested.

## Ethics and custody

Acknowledge community expertise through an agreed attribution/compensation arrangement; do not assume source provision establishes consent or reviewer authority. Obtain consent for the specific review task, identity processing, retention and attribution. A research lead must approve private storage, access list, retention period, withdrawal handling and incident contact before any restricted packet is assembled. No approved destination or retention decision has been supplied for this pilot. Publish only cleared aggregate methods/results; privately retain linkable evidence and identities. No recruitment or outreach is performed by this assignment.
