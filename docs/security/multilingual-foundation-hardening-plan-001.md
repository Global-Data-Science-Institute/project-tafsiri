013 leaves legacy grants/policies/views unchanged. Its own nine tables are private with RLS/no client policies. Ordinary clients cannot mutate profiles or scope assertions; only separately reviewed owner operations can do so. Registry reader grants are exact-scope, finite and revocable. Configuration/approval history is immutable or audited. No registry relationship grants authority. The restricted source-scope table has no client read function.

Remaining operational risks include privileged operator mistakes, database owner bypass, login-name reuse, incomplete audit after transaction abort, private snapshot custody and broad locking. Before deployment: approve the operator workflow, credential/TLS handling, access/retention and log policy; compare hosted schema/grants/history; verify backups/recovery; review legacy hardening or technically proven isolation. Include denied cross-language reads/writes, unavailable configuration, expired/revoked grants, private response-field absence and proposal-policy boundary tests.

No staging or production write is authorized. Any protected-data exposure or unauthorized-write finding remains a deployment blocker until remediated or safely isolated and independently tested. Do not dismiss a finding because it predates this migration.
